01 / THE ARTIST

About PHLORYDA

PHLORYDA, born Derick Chibueze Madu, is a Nigerian rapper, singer and songwriter from the eastern region of Nigeria, based in Rivers State.

His sound blends Igbo and English, drawing from Afro Drill and other African musical influences. His music explores survival, personal struggles, ambition and the realities of everyday life.

PHLORYDA represents more than music. The Flow Rider identity reflects resilience, determination and the instinct to keep moving through life's waves.
